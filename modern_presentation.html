Theoretical Foundations

Pedagogical Framework & Curriculum Alignment

🎯 Backward Design (UbD)

Derived from Wiggins & McTighe. Begin with the end in mind: Identify desired writing outcomes first, determine acceptable evidence of capability, and plan reading scaffolds accordingly.

📈 English Learning Activities View

Progression from Learning & Understanding (textual deconstruction) $\rightarrow$ Applying & Practicing (story map transformation) $\rightarrow$ Transferring & Creating (original narrative creation).

🌟 Four Core Competencies

Holistic integration of Language Ability (5W1H vocabulary), Thinking Quality (inferring tacit intentions), Cultural Awareness (family ethics), and Learning Ability.

🤖 Teaching-Assessment & AI Integration

Closed-loop evaluation: Generative AI (Jimeng AI) activates emotional schemata, while intelligent diagnostic tools (Qianwen App) offer instant formative evaluation and data-driven remediation.

Contextual Diagnostics

Material & Learner Analysis

Material Analysis (The Text)

Man & Society
  • • Thematic Theme: Family traditions, moral integrity, and parental guidance.
  • • Core Plot: Jim catches a trophy bass out of season; his father quietly insists he return it to the water, embedding a lifelong moral anchor.
  • • Linguistic Style: Abundant dynamic verbs, precise spatial-temporal transitions, and rich sensory descriptions.
[🖼️ Placeholder: Textbook Unit Cover & Highlighted Paragraphs]

Student Profile (Grade 10/11)

Learner State
  • • Cognitive Foundation: Familiar with past-tense narrative writing; basic grasp of chronological organization.
  • • Learning Gap: Tends to write superficial recounts without psychological depth or dramatic tension ("events without soul").
  • • Pedagogical Need: Requires explicit visual scaffolds (Fishing Rod Board) and structured prompts to extract deeper moral significance.
Key Remediation: Transition from "What happened" to "What it meant to me"
Intended Learning Outcomes

Teaching Objectives & Core Foci

1. Language Ability

Master narrative action verbs and descriptive adverbs; utilize the 5W1H structure to construct a well-paced story.

2. Thinking Quality

Infer the father’s unstated motives; critically interpret "personal integrity" when nobody is watching.

3. Cultural Awareness

Recognize how family rituals and parental principles serve as moral compasses across diverse cultural settings.

4. Learning Ability

Apply the visual Story Map to self-monitor narrative planning and embrace AI-assisted reflective revision.

Teaching Key Point: Deconstructing the story structure via 5W1H and extracting the underlying ethical value.
Teaching Difficult Point: Guiding students to elevate everyday personal experiences into enduring family principles.

Instructional Procedure

Step 3: Post-reading — Scaffolded Writing Transfer

Duration: 15 Mins
Sentence Starter 1 (Hook - Setting)

"It was a [crisp autumn / stormy] day. I was with [my father / mother] at [the quiet lakeside / our workshop]..."

Sentence Starter 2 (Line & Reel - Conflict)

"Suddenly, [the unexpected occurred]. I felt a surge of [excitement / conflict] because [I wanted to keep it, yet rules forbade it]..."

Sentence Starter 3 (Fish - Reflection)

"From this experience, I learned that [true character is revealed in quiet choices]. That moment became the true catch of my life."
